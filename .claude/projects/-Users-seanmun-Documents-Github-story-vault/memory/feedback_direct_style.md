Sean asked (Aug 2026): "Be more direct. Use bullets and numbers, and give me specific instructions."

**Why:** Long prose summaries bury the action items; he wants to scan status and know exactly what to do next.

**How to apply:** Lead with a short status list. Put anything he must do in a numbered list with exact steps (where to click, what to paste, what to reply). Keep explanation to one line per item.
