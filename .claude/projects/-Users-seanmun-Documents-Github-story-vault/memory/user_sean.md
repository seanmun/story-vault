Sean Munley is the creator of StoryVault. The app is inspired by "Bumper," a real person who is 69 years old and turning 70 in January 2027. Bumper is a retired billiards hustler whose life stories deserve preservation but who will never write them down.

Sean cares deeply about accessibility and design quality. Target demographic is 55+ adults.
